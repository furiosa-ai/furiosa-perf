{{ title }}

LLM inference performance is a key factor that determines both the quality and cost of AI services, yet making fair, apples-to-apples comparisons across hardware platforms remains challenging. Rapid changes in the software stack and hardware-specific optimization points quickly make static benchmarks diverge from real-world deployments.

In this report, we evaluate LLM inference on Furiosa AI's RNGD using the furiosa-llm, and on a range of GPUs using vLLM. For each hardware and sofrware platform, we systematically sweep tensor parallel sizes and concurrency levels, and present the results primarily from a throughput perspective to enable a comprehensive view beyond a single headline number. This approach is intended to highlight practical platform characteristics such as scalability, stability under load, and overall efficiency.
